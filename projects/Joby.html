Technical Program Manager – Flight Test Instrumentation Intern

  • Programming & Data: Python, SQL, PySpark, Databricks
  • Engineering Software: LabVIEW, Jira, Confluence, Smartsheet

Contributions

BOM Smartsheet Comparator

Developed an internal cross-functional tool that reconciled the source-of-truth aircraft BOM against eight engineering team Smartsheet schedules. The tool identified parts that were missing from schedules, incorrectly formatted, overdue, or not aligned with current design revisions.

  • Built Python, SQL, and PySpark data-processing tools in Databricks to standardize engineering schedule data from eight Smartsheet project trackers.
  • Developed an ETL pipeline that extracted part numbers, revisions, dates, completion percentages, assignees, and schedule information from inconsistent human-entered Smartsheet data.
  • Connected CATIA BOM data with Smartsheet schedules through Databricks, Lakebase/Postgres, a backend API, and a React frontend to create a continuously updated engineering view.
  • Created automated checks that identified unreleased parts missing from PM schedules, overdue work, revision mismatches, and incomplete schedule coverage.
  • Replaced a manually cross-referenced Excel process covering 15 BOMs and eight schedules with an automated workflow that could be run with a single click.
  • Added ownership, schedule source, engineering contact, maturity, start/finish dates, completion status, and revision information to improve accountability across engineering teams.
  • Deployed a Streamlit interface so non-technical program managers could generate reports without accessing the command line.
  • Impact: Reduced manual schedule and BOM reconciliation while giving program managers a centralized way to identify missing work, overdue designs, and schedule discrepancies.

Sensor Calibration Tracker

Digitized and automated the Flight Test Instrumentation team's sensor calibration records to improve accessibility, traceability, and compliance during aircraft flight testing.

  • Converted physical calibration records stored in the FTI team's inventory into a centralized digital database.
  • Developed a dashboard to track sensor model numbers, locations, purchasers, calibration dates, and aircraft assignments.
  • Implemented automated alerts for sensors approaching the three-month calibration threshold and requiring recalibration.
  • Allowed engineers to add, remove, and update sensors as instrumentation configurations changed during aircraft development and testing.
  • Improved traceability of sensor calibration records supporting flight test activities, FAA compliance, and aircraft Type Certification.
  • Impact: Replaced a physical-only recordkeeping process with an accessible and maintainable digital tracking system for flight-test instrumentation.

Aircraft Instrumentation & Mechanical Design

Supported the design and implementation of flight test instrumentation across SN101, SN102, SN103, and SN104, working with mechanical, electrical, structural, test, and aircraft technician teams.

  • Supported instrumentation installation and integration for strain gauges, accelerometers, OAT sensors, thermocouples, air data booms, and DAQ systems.
  • Performed GD&T reviews and engineering sanity checks on aircraft hardware and instrumentation drawings.
  • Supported the design of sensor brackets, braces, wire harness clamps, camera mounts, and other flight-test hardware.
  • Contributed to instrumentation configurations for SN102 and SN103, including DAQ chassis and harness clamp designs.
  • Performed structural calculations and engineering checks including stress, shear, moment, beam, and vibration analyses to support design validation.
  • Reviewed designs with structural and test engineers to verify manufacturability, structural feasibility, and installation requirements.
  • Worked directly with aircraft technicians to support hardware installation and verify installations against engineering drawings.

Technical Program Management

  • Managed and delegated engineering activities across a 20+ person FTI team, tracking priorities, deliverables, blockers, and program milestones.
  • Led weekly stand-ups and cross-functional meetings covering design progress, aircraft configurations, schedule risks, and engineering blockers.
  • Coordinated design reviews and engineering approvals across mechanical, electrical, structural, software, and test engineering teams.
  • Reviewed engineering change requests, CAD designs, drawings, BOMs, and code changes to ensure designs and engineering data remained aligned.
  • Held approval authority for engineering change requests affecting finalized or installed aircraft designs.
  • Escalated blockers, assigned appropriate engineering reviewers, and coordinated reviews to accelerate design timelines.
  • Supported procurement and manufacturing activities by ordering parts, tracking inventory, coordinating in-house manufacturing runs, and monitoring part delivery timelines.

Engineering Automation & Data Tools

  • Developed Python utilities to analyze flattened BOMs and identify which parts required procurement versus parts already available or unnecessary.
  • Built engineering dashboards and data-processing scripts to improve visibility into BOM releases, calibration records, inventory, schedules, and aircraft instrumentation.
  • Used Databricks, SQL, PySpark, and Python to move and transform engineering data between Smartsheet, Databricks, Lakebase/Postgres, backend services, and frontend applications.
  • Automated repetitive engineering workflows to reduce manual data entry and improve consistency across teams.
